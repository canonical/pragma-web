Why this model

A shared vocabulary for what the company builds, sells and delivers — governed row by row. Everything below comes straight from the working document.

Assumptions & axioms

A1Validated

The semantic model expresses an enterprise-sales-centered vision of the company: it distinguishes sales vs. non-sales readings of the same concept, prefers "enterprise sales" over generic "sales", and lets that preference drive modelling order, entity choice and default relation shape.

A2Proposal

The model should identify without deeply modelling non-catalog entities (actors, governance constraints, records). They serve as interfaces that strengthen the product model.

A3Proposal

Classes should be owned by a department, which keeps authority over the definition and evolution of its vocabulary.

H1Proposal

Non-asset entities considered: Governance/Constraints, Actors, Records/Events.

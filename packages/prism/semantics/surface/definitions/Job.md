# Job

A [Job](surface:Job) is one thing a consumer is trying to get done, written
as a story with four slots: when a circumstance arises, I want something, so
I can reach an outcome, without a pain. The story is the job's identity; its
acceptance clauses are how anyone can tell whether it is served.

Who has the job is its [coordinate region](surface:Coordinate), never a
persona: personas are derived by overlap. Whether it is served is read from
its [pairings](surface:Pairing). A job that no surface serves is a coverage
gap, unless it is [deferred](surface:deferred) with a
[wake condition](surface:wakeCondition) that says when it comes back.

Jobs also justify the parts of a page. A UI block such as the
[Card](ds:global.component.card) [serves](surface:serves) the jobs that need
it, and an optional [slot](surface:Slot) of a layout exists only because a
job [summons](surface:summonedBy) it.

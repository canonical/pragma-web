# Engineer

Builds applications with the design system's components. Their region is
the [engineer coordinate](dsd:coordinate.engineer): they
[learn props and usage](dsd:job.e1), [cite standards](dsd:job.e2) in review
and [jump to pages by name](dsd:job.e3). Their agents do the same work
through the [GraphQL port](dsd:port.graphql).

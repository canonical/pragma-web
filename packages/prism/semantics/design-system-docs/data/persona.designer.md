# Designer

Designs product screens with the design system. Their region is the
[designer coordinate](dsd:coordinate.designer), at any fluency, so their
jobs are [browsing the catalogue](dsd:job.l3),
[studying a component's anatomy](dsd:job.d1) and
[checking its tokens](dsd:job.d2), plus every job of a
[newcomer](dsd:coordinate.newcomer) and of a
[maker](dsd:coordinate.maker) as they grow.

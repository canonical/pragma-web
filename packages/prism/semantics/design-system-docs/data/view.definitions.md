# Definitions

The ontology explorer: every class of the graph's ontologies on one canvas,
with a rail of terms and an inspector. It serves the maintainer who
[places a class](dsd:job.m1), and the reader who follows a chip to
[define a term](dsd:job.l4).

It composes the [ApplicationLayout](ds:apps.layout.application_layout); the
inspector is a [DescriptionList](ds:global.pattern.description_list).

# DataViews — React

`@canonical/dataviews-react` shows a collection of records, and lets a reader search, filter, sort, select and page through it. The state lives in a provider from `@canonical/dataviews-core`. The React parts render that state and send the reader's commands back to it.

> **Experimental.** Every export is pre-release, and its shape may change between minor versions.

## How the parts fit

```
createDataViewsProvider   @canonical/dataviews-core: the collection, a source, and its ports
  └─ DataViews            the root: mounts the provider for the parts inside it
       ├─ controls        QueryBar · Search · Filters · SortPanel · Settings · SavedViews · Pagination · ActionBar
       └─ renderers       DataTable
```

- The **root**, `DataViews`, takes the provider once. Every part inside it reads the root and takes no provider of its own.
- The **controls** edit the query, the ordering, the arrangement and the selection, through the provider.
- The **renderers** show the records. `DataTable` is exported. `DataCards`, `DataChart` and `DataChartWithTable` are prototypes and are not exported from the package root.
- The **standalone blocks** work without a root: `PaginationBar` and `DataTable` take a provider, and `QueryInput`, `QueryChip`, `QueryChips`, `FilterButton` and `FilterOptions` take their values from the host.

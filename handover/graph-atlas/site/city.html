14 / 16 · the design system as a city

325 blocks as buildings, 16 tiers as districts

A picture for a slide. The ground plan is the tier tree: the global tier is the centre, the apps tier and its ten product tiers sit to its right, sites, documentation and stores below. Every block is a building; its height is how much the graph specifies about it; its colour is the grain. A solid building has an implementation in a library; an outlined one is specified but not yet built.

Hover a building for its name; click it for its details. "Copy as SVG" puts the drawing on the clipboard as vector, ready to paste into Keynote, Figma or Inkscape; a screenshot works too. Rotate turns the whole city a quarter turn so a different district faces the viewer.

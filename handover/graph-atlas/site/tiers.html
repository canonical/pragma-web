03 / 16 · tiers and blocks

313 blocks, 16 tiers, one chain of inheritance

A block is one reusable piece of interface at one of four grains: component, subcomponent, pattern or layout. Every block belongs to a tier, and tiers form a hierarchy: a product tier such as apps_lxd inherits the blocks of apps, which inherits those of global. Reads in pragma are scoped by that chain.

The inheritance chain

Tier IRIs encode their parent (apps_lxd sits under apps); the four top-level product families sit under global. The bar beside each tier is its own block count; a product tier sees its own blocks plus everything above it.

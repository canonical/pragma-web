25 frames, each one a real component

A board of frames, the way a design file lays out its components. Every frame holds the published React component, server-rendered to the markup it really produces and styled by its own stylesheet, so what you see is what ships. Frames take the size their component needs and pack into the grid; their elevation is the block's anatomy depth in the graph, so a component built from more parts floats higher. Open the index for navigation; click a frame for what the graph knows about it.

Move the pointer across the board to tilt it; switch the tilt off for a flat capture. The frames are live DOM: hover states, focus rings and the open accordion all work. Each frame is a Shadow DOM island that loads only pragma's stylesheet, so nothing from this page restyles the component. ContextualMenu and Popover need a browser-side hook context, and Chip and Label are not finalised; they are not on this board.
